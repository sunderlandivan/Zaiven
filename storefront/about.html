About us

Who we are

The PreEminent Store is a small US-based online trading card game shop. We've been selling to collectors and players since 2024, and you'll find us opening packs on our YouTube channel.

We carry sealed product and single cards for:

  • Pokémon
  • Magic: The Gathering
  • One Piece Card Game
  • Riftbound
  • Flesh and Blood
  • Weiss Schwarz
  • Gundam Card Game

Sealed product is factory sealed and sourced from distributors and retailers. Singles are listed with their condition so you know what you're getting.

We also sell custom decks that we build by hand from cards we pulled in our own openings. They're ready to play, but they're opened cards, not factory-sealed or official publisher products, and each deck's page says so.

We're an online-only shop (no retail storefront). Orders ship from California, and we also sell on TCGplayer and eBay.

How we ship

We ship within the United States only. Orders are packed within 1–2 business days and ship with tracking. Boxes are packed with padding so corners and seals arrive intact, and you'll get an email with your tracking number once your order ships.

Shipping is one flat rate per order. See shipping details and our returns policy (all sales are final; damaged or wrong items are made right).
