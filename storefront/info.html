Shipping

We ship within the United States only. Shipping is one flat rate per order, no matter how many items you buy:

  • Sealed product (booster boxes, ETBs, bundles, collections): $10.99
  • Custom decks (with no sealed product in the order): the deck rate shown on the deck's page
  • Single cards only: $1.49

The shipping charge is shown in your cart and again before you pay. There are no other fees.

Orders are packed within 1–2 business days and ship with tracking, usually arriving 2–5 business days after shipping. You'll get an email with your tracking number once it ships. Boxes are packed with padding so corners and seals arrive intact.

Sales tax

We collect sales tax only on orders shipped to California addresses, at the rate for the delivery address. Orders shipped to other states aren't charged sales tax by us. The exact tax, if any, is shown before you pay.

Returns & refunds

No returns. All sales are final. Because trading card products can be resealed, swapped or tampered with, we don't accept returns or exchanges on any item, including sealed product, singles and custom decks.

  • Damaged or wrong item: email us within 3 days of delivery with photos and we'll make it right with a replacement or full refund, including shipping. You don't need to send the item back unless we ask (and if we do, we pay the return shipping).
  • Lost package: if your package's tracking shows no movement for 10 days, email us and we'll send a replacement or a full refund.
  • Sold out: if an item sells out before we can ship it, we'll refund that item in full right away.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method and usually appear within 5–10 business days.

Cancellations

You can cancel for a full refund any time before your order ships. Email us with your order number. Once an order has shipped it can't be cancelled.

Unpaid Venmo and Zelle orders are cancelled automatically after 24 hours.

FAQ

Are your products authentic?

Yes. Sealed product is factory sealed and purchased from distributors and retailers. Single cards are listed with their condition. Custom decks are built by us from cards we opened ourselves, so they aren't factory sealed and aren't official publisher products.

Is the stock shown accurate?

Inventory updates automatically throughout the day. On rare occasions an item sells elsewhere at the same moment. If that happens, we refund that item immediately.

How do I pay?

Instant: through PayPal. Pay with your PayPal account, Venmo, or any major debit or credit card without needing a PayPal account. Payment happens in PayPal's secure window; we never see your card number.

Direct Venmo or Zelle: choose "Pay with Venmo" or "Pay with Zelle" at checkout and enter your shipping address. We reserve your items and show you (and email you) the exact amount and your order number. Send the payment to @ThePreEminentStore on Venmo, or with Zelle to the address on your order page (we email it too), and put your order number in the note or memo. On Venmo, choose "Purchase" if it asks.

  • We ship after your payment arrives (usually the same or next day).
  • Unpaid orders are cancelled after 24 hours and the items go back on sale.
  • Direct Venmo and Zelle are available for orders up to $2,500, shipped within the US. Prices and shipping are the same as PayPal.
  • We will never ask you to pay a different account or to "upgrade" a payment. If anyone does, it isn't us.

Can I pick up locally?

At this time all orders are shipped.

Do you buy collections or take preorders?

Reach out through the contact info below and we'll let you know.

Privacy policy

We collect only what's needed to fulfill your order: your name, email, shipping address and order details. Card and PayPal payments are handled by PayPal, and we never receive or store your card number. If you pay with Venmo or Zelle, we see the name and note on that payment so we can match it to your order.

We use your information to ship your order, send order and tracking emails, and answer your questions. We don't sell or share your information with anyone except the payment processor and shipping carrier needed to complete your order.

This site stores your cart in your own browser so it's there when you come back. We don't use advertising trackers. To ask about or delete your information, contact us.
