### Pokémon
- The Pokémon Company International (TPCi) doesn't sell to small retailers directly. Pokémon Center has **no business or wholesale accounts** ([Pokémon Center support](https://support.pokemoncenter.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000787103-Where-can-I-find-information-about-distributors)).
- Official US distributors: ACD, Universal US, GTS, Gold River, Mad Al, Peachstate/PHD, Southern Hobby, Sweet Deal, Square Root Games, Hamps, MichiganHobby, U & I, and Magazine Exchange ([Pokémon support list](https://support.pokemon.com/hc/es/articles/360001004653--Qui%C3%A9nes-son-sus-distribuidores-mayoristas)).
- **Allocation:** new accounts get little or nothing on hot sets. Gold River says it takes about **a year of consistent buying** before a new account shows up meaningfully in its allocation data. Mad Al bases allocation on 12-month spend and tenure. Plan on steady mid-size orders of evergreen product.
- Play! Pokémon Store status requires a public commercial venue with signage, a counter, and seating for 8, so it isn't an option for us ([pokemon.com](https://www.pokemon.com/us/play-pokemon/organize/event-requirements)).
- TPCi is acquiring Excell Brands, the largest US Pokémon distributor, but Excell serves mass-market retail, not small shops ([ICv2](https://icv2.com/print/article/61690)).

### Magic: The Gathering
- **WPN (Wizards Play Network) requires a brick-and-mortar store.** Online-only businesses aren't eligible, and the store can't be inside another business or under construction ([How to apply for WPN](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/news/how-apply-wpn), [askWPN](https://askwpn-na.wizards.com/hc/en-us/articles/13384467692435-What-is-required-to-join-the-WPN)).
- **Selling current sealed Magic online in North America requires Wizards' Authorized Internet Retailer (AIR) status**, and AIR requires an active WPN store at Core level or higher ([Wizards support](https://secretlair-support.wizards.com/hc/en-us/articles/5582208251412-Verify-You-are-Purchasing-Legitimate-Wizards-of-the-Coast-Products)). AIR stores also can't resell current sealed Magic to other businesses (see [Hills' terms](https://wholesalegaming.com/shipterm/) and [Da Card World](https://help.dacardworld.com/en/articles/881088)).
- **What this means for us:** without a storefront there is no legitimate route to *current* sealed Magic from authorized channels. Singles and out-of-print sealed products aren't covered by the AIR agreement. Ask ACD/GTS what Magic they'll sell to an online account; expect "singles and accessories only."

### Flesh and Blood
- Legend Story Studios' policy: **sealed Flesh and Blood may only be sold online by brick-and-mortar stores.** The online-only exception is for single-card websites that existed before Oct 2020 ([fabtcg.com retailer supply policy](https://fabtcg.com/resources/retailer-supply-policy/)).

### Riftbound / One Piece / Gundam
- Riftbound (UVS Games) authorized distributors in North America: Carta, ACD, Asmodee USA, GTS, PHD, Southern Hobby, and Universal ([Carta list](https://cartadistribution.com/distributors/riftbound), [UVS FAQ](https://uvsgames.com/riftbound-faq/)).
- One Piece official North American distributors: GTS, Universal, PHD, and Southern Hobby ([Bandai](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/forstore/)). In practice that leaves **GTS** as the only realistic option for us.

### Secondary, import, and accessory options
- **Secondary wholesalers / "case sellers"** (e.g. RSC/rs-chain.com, Spook): no allocation lottery, but prices are pegged near TCGplayer market, so thin margins. Use them for fill-in only, after a small test order.
- **Japanese/Asian importers** (e.g. TCG Akiba, Pokemon Supplier, HeroTCG for the EU): good for Japanese and Chinese product, which isn't covered by US allocation. Check counterfeit risk, tariffs, and minimums (HeroTCG: €499).
- **Accessories:** Ultra PRO has **no direct retailer accounts** and sells only through distributors ([Ultra PRO support](https://support.ultrapro.com/en-US/does-ultra-pro-sell-directly-to-hobby-retail-stores-380711)). Legion Supplies opens accounts with a state tax permit and EIN, but asks for a brick-and-mortar address. ACD and Gold River both carry Ultra PRO and BCW.

### Scams and red flags
- Telegram/Discord "suppliers" taking preorder money by wire, Zelle, or crypto. In Feb 2026, PawsTCG/Fulfillment TCG left 100+ buyers about S$700k out of pocket ([Mothership](https://mothership.sg/2026/02/pokemon-trading-cards-preorder-scam/)).
- **Re-labeled sealed cases**: fake Evolving Skies case labels on Journey Together cases, sold on eBay in 2026 ([Elite Fourum](https://www.elitefourum.com/t/fake-sealed-evolving-skies-booster-case-scam-in-2026/61102)). Inspect case stamps and tape, and buy cases only from distributors.
- Fake Shopify "TCG stores" pushed through paid ads, e.g. holobooster, poketurbo, pokelios, tcgora ([Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/fake-pokemon-card-stores-target-collectors/)).
- **Paid "supplier directory" sites** (e.g. tcgsupplierlist.com) recycle public information and push "no-license" sources. Their guide still calls Alliance a standalone distributor a year after the Universal buyout. Don't pay for lists.
- Rules of thumb: pay by credit card or net terms, never wire for preorders. Verify the business on the state Secretary of State registry. Insist on invoices. Be wary of "authorized" claims you can't confirm on the brand's own distributor list.

## 6. Website check — what distributors look for (checked 2026-10-06)

> A newer review (2026-10-07) is in `data/distributor-kit/site-review.md`. Since this list was written, the site gained an About page and hard-coded contact email. Still open: the personal Zelle payee, sealed Magic/Flesh and Blood listings, no phone, and no wholesale-inquiry line.

**Already good:** own domain, about 300 products with live stock, working checkout (PayPal plus Venmo/Zelle), shipping and returns/refunds, privacy policy, trademark disclaimer, YouTube/Instagram links, a business email on the domain, city and state shown.

**Gaps to fix before applying:**
1. **Contact email shows "(email coming soon)"** in the static HTML of `info.html`. It only becomes `sales@preeminentstore.com` after JavaScript loads the catalog, so a reviewer with JS blocked, a crawler, or a slow load sees "coming soon." Hard-code the address.
2. **No About page.** Add an "About us" page covering who runs the store, since when (2024), TCGplayer/eBay history, a photo of the fulfillment/inventory space, and "California seller's permit holder." Credit departments look for this first.
3. **No phone number** anywhere on the site.
4. **No legal entity name.** Add "© The PreEminent Store LLC" (or the exact registered name once the entity question is settled) to the footer and terms.
5. **No footer policy links.** Shipping, returns, privacy, and terms all live in one long info page that isn't linked separately in the footer. Add footer links to `info.html#shipping`, `#returns`, `#privacy`, plus a short Terms of Sale section.
6. **Single-card shipping mismatch** in static fallbacks: the homepage shows $1.49 and `info.html` shows $1.27 until JS updates both to the live $1.49. Make the static values match.
7. **Zelle payee is a personal email.** Checkout config sends Zelle to a personal Live.com address. Switch to the business address if Zelle allows, so payment instructions look like a business.
8. **Sealed Magic and Flesh and Blood listings.** Wizards says only Authorized Internet Retailers may sell current sealed Magic online, and LSS says sealed Flesh and Blood online is limited to brick-and-mortar stores. Distributors with WotC/LSS contracts (ACD included) may notice this on the site. Consider de-emphasizing current sealed Magic and Flesh and Blood (keep singles and out-of-print) while applications are under review.
9. Optional extras: a "Wholesale / business inquiries" contact line, a preorder policy, and a "Factory sealed, sourced from authorized distributors" badge once an account is open.
