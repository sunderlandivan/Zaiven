Privacy Policy

The PreEminent Store - Portal · Last updated October 1, 2026

Who uses this app

The PreEminent Store Portal is a private tool used only by the owner of The PreEminent Store to manage the store's inventory. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data is accessed

  • Gmail (read-only): the app reads order, purchase and sale confirmation emails to find the items bought or sold, their quantities and prices. It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Google Sheets: the app reads and updates the store's own inventory spreadsheet (adding purchased items and logging sales).

How the data is used and stored

  • The app runs on the owner's own computer. Data read from Gmail is used only to update the store's inventory sheet.
  • Sign-in tokens and a small record of processed orders are stored only on that computer.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data is used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
