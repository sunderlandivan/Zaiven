# Distributor leads, round 2: sources that may take an online-only store

Researched 2026-10-08. Nothing was sent, submitted, or signed up for. This is a research list only.

Excluded because they were already contacted (see `docs/distributor-plan.md` and the private kit README): ACD, Hills, Carta, Gold River, PHD/Peachstate, Universal US, Mad Al, Southern Hobby, GTS, Sweet Deal.

**The short version:** only one remaining authorized US Pokémon distributor looks like it might take an online store, and that's MichiganHobby. Two more on Pokémon's list (Magazine Exchange and Hamps) haven't published a policy, so call them. No authorized source anywhere sells current sealed Magic, Flesh and Blood, One Piece (English), Gundam or Lorcana to an online-only shop. ACD (already applied) and Carta (already applied) are the only open routes for those. Beyond that, the realistic sources are accessories direct from BCW, Japanese-import wholesalers, and secondary wholesalers that charge near market price.

## Ranked leads

Ranked by how likely they are to accept an online-only store first, then by fit (Pokémon/Magic first).

### 1. MichiganHobby (Wixom, MI): best remaining shot at authorized Pokémon

- **Website:** https://www.michiganhobby.com · sales@michiganhobby.com · 1-855-644-6229
- **Lines:** Pokémon (listed on Pokémon's official US distributor list; the CEO's LinkedIn says "Direct Distribution Partners of The Pokémon Company International, Fanatics (Topps) and Upper Deck"), sports cards, toys, board games.
- **Online-only accepted?** **Unclear, leaning possible.** Its model is "non-hobby retail" (convenience, grocery, toy and gift stores). It doesn't say online stores are excluded, and its contact page quotes a testimonial from "Jacob, E-Commerce Store Owner." The homepage counts "Brick-N-Mortar Locations," so expect questions. Sources: [home](https://www.michiganhobby.com/), [contact](https://www.michiganhobby.com/contact-us/), [about](https://www.michiganhobby.com/about-us/), [CEO LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/michiganhobby).
- **How to apply:** Homepage "Join the waitlist" (2026 waitlist) or the survey on https://www.michiganhobby.com/wholesale/ ("a representative will be in touch within 48 hours"). Then they send an application, a credit-card authorization form and a business-entity form, which go back to sales@michiganhobby.com ([Our Thought page](https://www.michiganhobby.com/our-thought/)).
- **Minimums/terms:** No minimum quantity and no minimum order amount. Payment goes through Square/Block or PayPal ([terms](https://www.michiganhobby.com/terms-of-use/)).
- **Real business?** Yes. It has operated since Feb 2019, has a named CEO (Jeff Turk), is on Pokémon's distributor list, and has a consistent site, phone and email.
- **Verdict:** Apply first. It's the only authorized Pokémon distributor found whose stated model isn't "hobby game store only."

### 2. Magazine Exchange / "MagX" (Grants Pass, OR): authorized Pokémon and Magic distributor, policy unclear

- **Website:** https://magazine-exchange.com (showed "under maintenance" on 2026-10-08) · 800-234-2004 / 541-479-7389 · phones Mon–Thu 9–5 PT, Fri until 4 PT
- **Lines:** Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, D&D, Funko, Upper Deck/Panini/Topps sports, Ultra PRO, Dragon Shield, Ultimate Guard, Max Protection. In business since 1976 and on Pokémon's US distributor list ([Grants Pass Chamber listing](https://business.grantspasschamber.org/list/member/magazine-exchange-inc-10403), [specials page](https://magazine-exchange.com/specials)).
- **Online-only accepted?** **Unclear.** Its specials page says some items are "available to our brick-and-mortar hobby partners" and "some items may be allocated and class of trade restrictions apply." That suggests it has more than one class of account (possibly including online), but restricts some product to stores. Expect no current sealed Magic for an online account (Wizards' Authorized Internet Retailer rule).
- **How to apply:** The site has "Create Account." Call a sales rep first and ask whether they open accounts for online retailers with their own website.
- **Minimums/terms:** $5 per carton UPS Ground (lower 48) on qualifying in-stock orders of $100+ ([specials](https://magazine-exchange.com/specials)). Other terms unknown.
- **Real business?** Yes: 50 years in business, a chamber of commerce member, and a physical warehouse at 2165 NE Industry Dr, Grants Pass.
- **Verdict:** Call this week. It carries both Pokémon and Magic and has cheap freight. Worth one phone call to learn the online policy.

### 3. Hamps Supply (Dallas, TX): authorized Pokémon distributor, phone only

- **Website:** none found · (214) 553-5545 · 10231 Plano Rd Suite 110, Dallas, TX 75238
- **Lines:** "Distributor of gaming and sports collectibles (wholesale only, not open to the public)" ([BBB](https://www.bbb.org/us/tx/dallas/profile/wholesale-novelties/hamps-supply-0875-90008421)). Listed on Pokémon's US distributor list.
- **Online-only accepted?** **Unclear.** No public policy.
- **How to apply:** Call. Ask whether they take online retailers and whether new accounts get Pokémon.
- **Minimums/terms:** Unknown.
- **Real business?** Yes: a BBB profile, a long-standing Dallas address, and a named owner (John Hoover). It's a small operation (about 3–9 staff).
- **Verdict:** A cheap phone call. It's small, so it may be flexible, or may have no Pokémon to spare for new accounts.

### 4. BCW Supplies (Middletown, IN): accessories direct, explicitly online-friendly

- **Website:** https://www.bcwsupplies.com/wholesale · 1-800-433-4229
- **Lines:** BCW sleeves, toploaders, card boxes, binders, graded-card storage and shipping supplies. Not sealed TCG.
- **Online-only accepted?** **Yes.** "Whether you run a local game store, an online collectibles shop, or a mix of both…" ([Proven Process Wholesale](https://www.bcwsupplies.com/proven-process-wholesale)). The FAQ even explains how eBay-store applicants are judged ([FAQ](https://www.bcwsupplies.com/faq/)).
- **How to apply:** The online Wholesale Application on bcwsupplies.com. Most are approved within 1 business day.
- **Minimums/terms:** No minimum order and no case quantity required (case quantity gets the best price). Prepay by card, PayPal, check or wire; credit terms by application. Requirements: a resale certificate, business license, or EIN ([retailer policy PDF](https://www.bcwsupplies.com/downloads/retail/DP-Policy.pdf)). **Common denial reasons:** a blanket resale certificate (fill one out naming BCW), a business address that doesn't match the certificate, a website that doesn't resolve, an eBay store whose name doesn't match the certificate, or an eBay rating under 90%.
- **Real business?** Yes. It's a major US accessories maker (Diversified, Inc.).
- **Verdict:** Easy approval. It replaces buying BCW supplies at retail and won't affect any distributor application.

### 5. KING PUNCH TCG (Osaka, Japan): Japanese Pokémon and One Piece, built for online stores

- **Website:** https://king-punch.net
- **Lines:** Japanese-language Pokémon, One Piece, Yu-Gi-Oh! OCG and Dragon Ball Fusion World booster boxes (current and back catalog).
- **Online-only accepted?** **Yes.** "If you run a shop, an online store or a distribution business, we'd be glad to hear from you." Business buyers only; it serves 46 countries including the US ([king-punch.net FAQ](https://king-punch.net/)).
- **How to apply:** One online form (about 5 minutes) and a "Trading Review" of up to 5 business days. **US sole proprietors must upload one business document**; use the seller's permit, since the permit is in Ivan's name.
- **Minimums/terms:** ¥200,000 minimum per order. The first order must be ¥200,000–500,000 (very roughly $1,300–3,300 depending on the exchange rate). 100% prepayment by Wise (no fee), bank transfer (+0.5%/1.5%) or PayPal (+5%, up to ¥1M). Invoices due within 24 hours. FedEx from Japan within 2 business days of payment. **US orders ship DDP with a 13% surcharge that covers US duties and taxes.**
- **Real business?** Likely. It publishes an Osaka address and a secondhand dealer license number (No. 62107R071362, Osaka Public Safety Commission), and says it sources from "established domestic distribution channels." Before the first order, check the license number and do a minimum-size first order.
- **Verdict:** The best-structured Japanese source found. Use it for Japanese Pokémon and One Piece, which US allocation doesn't cover.

### 6. Pokedot Games (Porter Ranch / Los Angeles, CA): in-state Japanese, Chinese and English Pokémon and One Piece

- **Website:** https://pokedotgames.com/pages/wholesale · info@pokedotgames.com
- **Lines:** Japanese, Chinese and English Pokémon; One Piece.
- **Online-only accepted?** **Yes.** "We supply sealed Pokémon and TCG products to retailers, livestream sellers, and resellers across the U.S." The application asks for business type, including "online seller" ([wholesale page](https://pokedotgames.com/pages/wholesale)).
- **How to apply:** Message form on the wholesale page with business name, state, type, website, estimated monthly volume and resale certificate. Reply within 1–2 business days.
- **Minimums/terms:** Not published. Approved partners get a weekly inventory email and "priority allocation on new products."
- **Real business?** Yes. Pokedotgames LLC is active with the CA Secretary of State (filed 2024-06-04, good standing, [bizprofile summary](https://www.bizprofile.net/ca/los-angeles/pokedotgames-llc)). Its Whatnot store has a 5.0 rating from 12,672 reviews and 66K items sold ([Whatnot](https://www.whatnot.com/user/pokedotgames)).
- **Verdict:** A good in-state fill-in source. It's a reseller rather than a distributor, so margins will be thinner, but it's in California, so in-state resale paperwork is simple.

### 7. Japanese-TCG.com (Tucson, AZ and Ohio): duties-paid Japanese cases with US stock

- **Website:** https://japanese-tcg.com
- **Lines:** Japanese Pokémon, One Piece, Weiss Schwarz and Union Arena cases and boxes, plus promo packs.
- **Online-only accepted?** **Yes.** No account needed; it sells by quote to "local card shops," "rip and ship streamers" and collectors ([home page](https://japanese-tcg.com/)).
- **How to apply:** Build a quote on the site and send the quote key to the owners. They confirm stock and send a payment link.
- **Minimums/terms:** Free shipping on any case or 6+ boxes; $25 flat for 1–5 boxes from Japan. **Prices include import duties and tariffs.** US stock ships from Ohio (1–2 days) or Arizona (2–5 days). Payment by Wise, wire, Zelle, Venmo, Cash App or PayPal.
- **Real business?** Probably. Named veteran owners (BCarpTCG and MASK Merch TCG / Skarpone) and "since 2023," but I found **no independent reviews or entity filing.** Pay **only by PayPal Goods & Services**, not Zelle, Venmo, Cash App or wire, and start with one case.
- **Verdict:** Useful if you want duties-paid pricing with no paperwork, but only after a small test order.

### 8. RSC / Lumination Ltd Co (Frisco, TX): English Pokémon at market price, use with caution

- **Website:** https://partners.rs-chain.com · support@rs-chain.com
- **Lines:** English sealed Pokémon (boxes, bundles, ETBs, cases), plus some sports cards.
- **Online-only accepted?** **Yes.** It accepts "online resellers (Whatnot, TikTok Shop, eBay, Cardmarket, Amazon)," and says a website is "the fastest route to approval" ([FAQ](https://partners.rs-chain.com/faq), [apply](https://partners.rs-chain.com/apply)).
- **How to apply:** An 11-step online application, reviewed within 24 hours.
- **Minimums/terms:** **Orders start at $5,000.** Prices are pegged to TCGplayer market, so margin is thin. Free FedEx shipping. **Every sale is final, with no returns** ([terms](https://partners.rs-chain.com/legal/terms)). Ships tax-exempt with a resale certificate on file. Net 15/30 only for established partners.
- **Real business?** Real but unproven. It's a registered Texas LLC, and BBB lists it at 5878 Bradley Ct, Frisco, as **not accredited with 3 complaints** ([BBB](https://www.bbb.org/us/tx/frisco/profile/online-shopping/lumination-0875-91232912)). I found no independent buyer reviews.
- **Verdict:** Last resort. The $5K minimum, final-sale terms and market-pegged pricing make it a poor fit at our volume.

| **Wizards Play Network (Magic)** | **No** | WPN needs a brick-and-mortar store. Selling *current* sealed Magic online needs Authorized Internet Retailer status, which needs WPN Core. ## Suggested order

1. **MichiganHobby:** join the 2026 waitlist or fill in the survey.
2. **Magazine Exchange and Hamps:** one phone call each (no email or forms needed to get the answer).
3. **BCW:** apply online with a CDTFA-230 that names BCW as the vendor.
4. **KING PUNCH and/or Pokedot:** only if Japanese Pokémon and One Piece are worth stocking. Start with a minimum-size first order.
5. **Japanese-TCG.com and RSC:** fill-in only, after a small test order and with PayPal Goods & Services or a card.

Before any of these, check that the customer agreement doesn't ban reselling to other businesses (transshipping), and keep every invoice as proof of where the product came from.
