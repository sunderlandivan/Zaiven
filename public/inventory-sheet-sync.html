Sheet Sync

Turns purchase and sale emails into updates on the Google inventory sheet.

Reads purchase emails (Pokemon Center, Target, Walmart, Costco, Amazon, TCGplayer, distributors) and sale emails (TCGplayer, eBay) from your Gmail, then updates the Poke Inventory Google Sheet: purchases raise Qty or add rows in Inventory26; sales add a row to Sold 2026 and lower Qty on the matched item. Confident matches are written automatically; anything unsure waits below for you.

Connection

One-time setup checklist
  1. Sign in below with the account that owns or can edit the Poke Inventory sheet (iv3nsun@gmail.com).
  2. In Google Cloud Console (same project as your Gmail client ID), enable the Google Sheets API.
  3. On the OAuth consent screen, set publishing status to In production so the login doesn't expire every 7 days.
  4. Click Connect Google above, sign in as iv3nsun@gmail.com, and approve Gmail + Sheets.
  5. Click Test sheet access, it should list Inventory26, Sold 2026 and Inventory25.
