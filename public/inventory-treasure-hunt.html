Treasure Hunt

Find products selling below what you paid, so you can restock for less.

How this works

Auto-scan walks your in-stock import and checks a fresh TCGplayer list price for each line (same logic as “Refresh market prices”). It estimates your landed cost using the tax % and flat shipping you enter (applied to that list price), then flags a hit if landed is far enough below your recorded unit cost. Only TCGplayer gets a live list price in this flow, so hits show TCGplayer as the vendor. Use Quick store search to check other sellers yourself. Manual deals — log a link and price you found anywhere; we compare to your sheet cost. Always confirm the listing and seller before buying.

Auto-scan (TCGplayer vs your cost)

The server also runs this scan automatically in the background (first time a few minutes after you start it, then on a long interval) so you usually do not need to press Run. Each run uses the same fixed rules (below), scans every in-stock line with unit cost (quantity ≥ 1, unit cost > 0), with a short pause per row so TCGplayer is not hammered — large sheets can take a while. When hits are found, the Treasure Hunt link in the sidebar flashes with stars until you click Stop flashing below. To turn passive scans off, set environment variable TREASURE_PASSIVE_SCAN=0 before starting the server.

Fixed scan rules: all eligible lines · landed must be at least 8% below your unit cost · 8% estimated tax on list price · $10.99 flat shipping per unit (landed = list × 1.08 + 10.99).
