Marketing

Review drafted social posts, make promo videos, and handle website orders.

Your marketing agent drafts posts and ideas every day. Edit anything, then Approve or Reject; approvals and rejections teach it what you like. After you post something, mark it Posted. The agent is told never to reveal exact stock or suggest prices below your 10% profit floor.

Drafts

Promo Shorts

Makes a 12-second animated vertical video (YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels): a joke opener, the product slamming in, a price stamp, a funny sticker, and the store link, with sound effects built in. Leave the joke and sticker blank for a random one, or write your own (use a new line for the punchline). Add a trending song in the YouTube or Instagram app when you upload.

Weekly Shorts (YouTube + Instagram)

Once a week the portal makes a new Short in the same flashy style as your approved 30th Celebration Short, featuring a rotating in-stock item (good-value items first, at the site's current price, never changed), then posts it to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. "Ask me first" waits for your Approve; the very first one always waits. Sound is generated effects only, so no copyright claims. Setup steps: docs/weekly-shorts.md.

Limited items

Website orders

"Came from" shows the link the buyer used, like youtube or ig-bio, so you can see which posts and videos sell. Zelle and Venmo orders hold their stock for 24 hours; ship them only after you mark them paid.

Scam check: Only mark paid after you see it in your bank app (Zelle) or Venmo app, with the order number in the memo. Ignore emails or texts claiming a Zelle or Venmo payment is "pending" or needs you to do something.

California sales tax

Paid website orders by month, for your CDTFA return. Tax collected is not income: "Total sales" leaves it out, just like the Sold sheet. Shipping on CA orders is untaxed only up to what the postage actually cost; any amount charged above the postage is taxable and goes on the return.
