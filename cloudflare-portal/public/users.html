Users & Audit

Who can sign in

Only the Google accounts listed here can open the portal. Viewers see exactly what staff see but can't change anything (the server refuses every change from a viewer account). Staff see web orders, order tracking (packing and shipping) and stock quantities, but no costs, profit, totals, tax, payouts, settings or payment details. Managers also see prices, costs and profit, Sold 2026, Sheet Sync, analytics and marketing, but not tax prep, loan, store settings, users or PayPal details. Removing someone or changing their role signs them out on their next click.

Add or change an employee

They sign in at portal.preeminentstore.com with that Google account. If Google says the app is in testing, add their email as a test user on the OAuth consent screen in Google Cloud.

Signed-in sessions

Sessions end after 12 hours, or sooner when idle.
